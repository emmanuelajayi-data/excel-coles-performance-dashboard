# 🛒 Coles Performance Dashboard (Excel)

An advanced Excel business analytics dashboard designed to monitor retail performance, sales trends, regional distribution, and financial profitability.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** ₦ 40,249,900
- **Total Profit:** ₦ 8,655,900
- **Total Orders:** 45
- **Total Units Sold:** 559

## 🔍 Key Features & Visualizations
- **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Line chart mapping revenue progression over time.
- **Top 5 Products by Revenue:** Horizontal bar chart highlighting top-performing merchandise.
- **Payment Method Distribution:** Pie chart analyzing customer transaction preferences across Card, Cash, and Transfer.
- **Salesperson & Regional Breakdown:** Comparative bar charts evaluating revenue performance across sales reps and cities.
- **Category Analysis:** Visual breakdown of revenue contributions across product categories.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Fully functional slicers enabling dynamic filtering by City, Salesperson, Product, Payment Method, Months, and Category.

## 🛠️ Technical Skills & Tools Used
- **Advanced Excel:** Spreadsheet architecture, data modeling, and dynamic range structuring.
- **Pivot Tables & Aggregations:** Clean data summarization to feed multi-component charts.
- **Data Visualization & UX:** Executive color-coding, custom KPI card formatting, and intuitive dashboard layout.
